    How you play

    Your current settings, as they are in the game's menu

    The curve set in your game's controller menu.
    From your mouse software. Not sure? Measure it with three swipes along a bank card (3.37 in), about 20 seconds.
    Fine-tuneOptional: turn speed check, deadzone, field of view, aim-down-sights and boost settings. Leave it closed and the range uses your game's defaults.
    1. In your game, find an open spot and pick a landmark (a tree, a building edge).
    2. Turn off any turning boost for now.
    3. Push the right stick all the way to the side and keep it there. Start the stopwatch as the landmark passes the crosshair.
    4. Count the landmark going by. When it passes for the 5th time, stop the stopwatch.
    5. Type the seconds here, with your sensitivity filled in above. Then put your boost back.

    Optional. Skip it and the range uses a built-in estimate of your game's turn speed. Do it and the range turns exactly as fast as your game. Five spins keeps a late tap on the stopwatch to a tiny error.
